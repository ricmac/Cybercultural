By 1996 the web was mainstream enough for Microsoft to attack it, with ActiveX and JScript, and for designers to split into two camps — Flash on one side, the brand-new CSS standard on the other.

It was also the year music went online in earnest, via RealAudio. See [What the Internet Was Like in 1996](/p/internet-1996/) for the overview.
