December 1968 is where a lot of computing history starts: Douglas Engelbart stood up in a San Francisco auditorium and showed a live, networked, interactive computer system to an audience that had never seen a mouse, a hyperlink or a video call.

The article tagged 1968 tells that story, and it continues in [1969](/tags/1969/), when Engelbart's team at the Augmentation Research Center built the working system behind the demo.
