The AOL–Time Warner merger in January 2000 marked the top of the market, and the crash began in March. The web kept developing regardless: blogrolls, Slashdot karma, the first RSS format war, and Apple quietly buying the company behind iTunes.

[What the Internet Was Like in 2000](/p/internet-2000/) covers the year as a whole.
