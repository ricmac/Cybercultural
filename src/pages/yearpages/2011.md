2011 was a year of power plays: Google+ took on Facebook, cloud storage and streaming became real businesses, and the Web 2.0 Summit was held for the last time.

It is also the year the ReadWriteWeb memoir reaches its ending, with the sale of the blog to SAY Media in December. Overview: [What the Internet Was Like in 2011](/p/internet-2011/).
