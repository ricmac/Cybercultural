1991 is the year the web left CERN and mostly failed to impress anyone. Berners-Lee took it to the Hypertext '91 conference hoping to win over the established hypertext research community, and was given a table rather than a stage.

The story picks up in [1992](/tags/1992/), when the first browsers written outside CERN appeared.
