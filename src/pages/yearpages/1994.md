1994 was the year the web turned into a mass medium: Netscape Navigator shipped in December, Yahoo grew out of a Stanford bookmark list, and designers started arguing about whether authors should control how a page looks.

It was also the last big year for the CD-ROM. For the overview, read [What the Internet Was Like in 1994](/p/internet-1994/); the {{ collections[title] | length }} articles tagged 1994 are below.
