1997 was the year of DHTML and of the first browser war turning nasty, with sites carrying “best experienced with” badges because they genuinely only worked in one browser. Web design split three ways between Zeldman, Siegel and Nielsen, and video streaming arrived in the form of a lot of buffering.

[What the Internet Was Like in 1997](/p/internet-1997/) is the overview; nine articles are tagged 1997.
