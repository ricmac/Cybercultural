2001 was the bottom of the dot-com bust and, oddly, one of the better years for the web itself: Wikipedia and the Wayback Machine both launched, iTunes made digital music legal, and the blogosphere was reshaped by the warblogs that followed September 11.

See [What the Internet Was Like in 2001](/p/internet-2001/) for the full picture.
