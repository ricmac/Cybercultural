1998 sits in the middle of the dot-com boom: portals advertised on television, Netscape open-sourced its browser as Mozilla, Amazon started selling CDs, and Google turned from a Stanford project into a company nobody had heard of yet.

It is also the year David Bowie launched BowieNet, a subject Cybercultural has covered at length. Overview: [What the Internet Was Like in 1998](/p/internet-1998/).
